## The Duality of Measurements

While the **International System of Units (SI)** is the scientific standard in most of the world, the **Imperial System** (or *US Customary*) remains predominant in the United States and specific industries, such as aeronautics and screen manufacturing (inches).

### Exact Conversion Factors

Since the "International Yard and Pound Agreement" of 1959, imperial units have been standardized based on the metric system to avoid ambiguity:

| Imperial Unit | Metric Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1 Inch (in)** | 25.4 mm (exact) |
| **1 Foot (ft)** | 30.48 cm |
| **1 Yard (yd)** | 0.9144 m |
| **1 Mile (mi)** | 1,609.344 m |

### Fun Fact: The Mars Climate Orbiter Error
In 1999, NASA lost a $125 million space probe because one engineering team used the metric system while another used the imperial system in navigation software. This reinforces the importance of reliable conversion tools like **Scalar**.

## How to convert manually?

If you need to perform a quick conversion without internet access, memorize these key factors:

1.  **From Inches to Millimeters:** Multiply the value by **25.4**.
2.  **From Feet to Meters:** Multiply by **0.3048**.
3.  **From Miles to Kilometers:** Multiply by approximately **1.609**.
