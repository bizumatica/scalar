At **Scalar**, our users' privacy is a top priority. As a technical utility platform, we operate under the following strict principles:

### 1. Local Data Processing
All calculation and utility tools (such as Base Converters, Fractions, and Units) are executed **exclusively within your browser**. The data you enter is never sent to our servers, is not stored, and is never shared with third parties.

### 2. Google AdSense and Cookies
We use Google AdSense to display advertisements and sustain the platform. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

### 3. External Links
Our website contains links to external professional profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn) and the author's personal portfolio. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of these third-party sites.

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or require technical support, please contact us via email at: **julio@bizumatica.com.br**.
